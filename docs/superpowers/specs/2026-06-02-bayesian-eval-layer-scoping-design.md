## 1. The question this answers

> Can a continuous-evidence / Bayesian evaluation layer **legitimately improve the deploy decision** for a modest (~1–1.5 annualized Sharpe) BTC edge — raise confirmation power, or improve the decision under low power — **WITHOUT spending the immutable 2024/2025 holdout and without a garden-of-forking-paths inflation** — by enough to justify building it? Or would such a layer be rigor-theater / framework-shopping on a frame that just earned four negatives?

The honest analytical frame that governs the whole study: **a Bayesian/continuous-evidence layer cannot manufacture statistical power on a fixed window — there is no free lunch on the signal-to-noise of the data.** The ~4.63/6.22 MDE wall is a noise hurdle set by window length `T` and multiplicity `N*` (cost-independent; established in the gate-power study). So the study's real job is to separate, mechanism by mechanism, **genuine value** (a different estimand with genuinely different power; an optional-stopping-proof accrual discipline; honest under-power reporting) from **illusory value** (re-labeling the same low power as a posterior; pooling that shrinks toward a negative cohort).

## 2. Hard framing — what keeps this cheap and clean (the "A⁺" boundary)

- **Reasoning-led, with small read-only inline verification.** The deliverable is a design/scoping spec that reaches a verdict by *reasoning*, and **spot-verifies each load-bearing quantitative claim with minimal read-only code** (closed-form checks or *tiny* synthetic/MC reusing existing primitives in `backtest/eval_power.py` / `backtest/eval_power_mc.py`). This hybrid is deliberate: the gate-power study's own lesson (METHODOLOGY §40) is that a clean-looking analytical projection can be confidently wrong — the Monte-Carlo caught two real bugs. Each spot-check is *subordinate to one specific claim* and stays minimal.
- **No production layer built.** This register produces the verdict + (if BUILD/CONDITIONAL) the design and the next-register recommendation — **never the production evaluation layer.** That is a separate downstream register.
- **No data spent, no peek, no holdout touched.** 2024 (validation) and 2025 (test) stay UNSPENT. No strategy is run on held data. Spot-checks use synthetic draws or the *already-sealed* Path-cycle moment summaries — never held-out performance.
- **Anti-scope-creep valve.** If a verdict genuinely hinges on a *large* power-comparison sweep exceeding the inline budget, that study is **named as the recommended next register** (→ CONDITIONAL verdict), not silently absorbed here.
- **Sealed-artifact safety.** `tier6_dsr_v1/` SHA-256 verified byte-unchanged before & after (every cycle does this). Read-only against all sealed artifacts; recompute, never rewrite. No call to `evaluate_cohort()` or any writer into a sealed directory.

## 3. Scope — the seven candidate mechanisms

A "continuous-evidence / Bayesian evaluation layer" is not one thing. The study adjudicates seven distinct mechanisms (M5–M7 added at the design-point B2). Each is classified per §5; none is pre-judged, though §4 records a held-loosely prior per mechanism.

- **M1 — Posterior-over-Sharpe → continuous (fractional-Kelly) sizing** *(the C decision-theoretic lens).* Replace the binary `deflated_z ≥ Z_PASS` gate with a posterior over true Sharpe; *size* allocation ∝ evidence rather than deploy-all / reject. Claimed value: avoids the all-or-nothing cliff. Failure mode: for a single candidate whose posterior straddles zero, "size ∝ evidence" ≈ size ≈ 0, and it puts real capital on noise; only harvests EV across a *stream* of many bets (not available to a solo with no candidate book). Does not *confirm* — relocates the problem to capital allocation (a Phase-3 concern).
- **M2 — Sequential / accruing Bayesian posterior** *(the Bayesian wrapper on "let `forward_2026` accrue").* A pre-registered sequential posterior over genuinely-fresh accruing OOS, with deploy / kill / keep-waiting boundaries. Claimed value: the one legitimate path to BUILD-viability without spending the holdout (power from `T` growing on never-touched tape) + early-kill. Failure mode: optional-stopping inflation unless paired with always-valid inference (→ M5); does **not** accelerate (power is from `T`, not Bayes).
- **M3 — Hierarchical / partial pooling across the (negative) cycles** *(the seed "pools across cycles").* Model candidates' true Sharpes as draws from a common distribution; pool to soften the FWER multiplicity penalty. Claimed value: shrinkage beats Bonferroni *when the cohort is a mix of positives and nulls*. Decisive failure mode here: the cohort (Paths B/A/C/D + the dead-18) is **uniformly negative** → partial pooling shrinks a new candidate **toward a group mean ≈ 0/negative** → makes confirmation *harder*. Borrowed "strength" = evidence of no edge.
- **M4 — Skeptical-prior calibration + posterior reporting** *(the seed "degrades gracefully under low power").* Encode accumulated evidence as a calibrated *skeptical* prior; report the posterior (continuous belief) instead of a binary pass/fail. Claimed value: distinguishes "wide near zero = *can't tell* (under-powered)" from "tight at zero = *no edge*" — genuine epistemic value. Failure mode: an honesty upgrade, **not** a power upgrade; a skeptical prior makes the posterior *more* conservative.
- **M5 — Always-valid / e-value / mSPRT inference** *(split from M2 at the B2).* A *frequentist martingale* framework (e-processes, anytime-valid confidence sequences, mixture SPRT), distinct from Bayesian posterior updating, that is **valid under continuous optional stopping**. Claimed value: it is the actual garden-of-forking-paths defense — for a solo researcher whose real risk is optional-stopping self-deception, it licenses honest continuous monitoring of accruing OOS. Failure mode: it does not create fixed-horizon power (an e-value cannot reach its threshold faster than evidence accrues).
- **M6 — Change the estimand** *(directional hit-rate / Brier vs Sharpe; added at the B2).* Evaluate confirmation on a *different test statistic* than the Sharpe-DSR. Claimed value: a directional-accuracy / proper-scoring statistic over many bars can have *materially different (sometimes higher) power* than Sharpe for a weak *persistent* edge, because it is not killed by fat-tailed P&L variance. **This is the lead most able to legitimately overturn DON'T-BUILD** — and the one the study must *measure*, not assume. Failure mode: a different estimand answers a *different* question (direction ≠ risk-adjusted return); its decision-value depends on whether directional confirmation is what deployment needs.
- **M7 — Within-batch empirical Bayes** *(winner's-curse correction on *future* cohorts; added at the B2).* Empirical-Bayes shrinkage across a *future exchangeable batch* of candidates (NOT the dead cycles — materially different from M3), to de-bias the selected winner. Claimed value: a legitimate selection-bias correction *if* a future candidate batch is ever in scope. Failure mode: conditional on a future batch existing; out of scope if the program stays frozen.

**Explicitly out of scope (stated to forestall a "gap" critique):** regime-switching priors and factor-model priors are *alpha-model* mechanisms, not evaluation-layer mechanisms — they belong to a signal cycle, not here.

## 4. Method — per-mechanism analysis + the spot-checks

For each mechanism the study (i) states the value-source and failure mode, (ii) classifies it on the two §5 axes, and (iii) where a load-bearing claim is quantitative, **verifies it with a minimal read-only computation**. Spot-checks (all read-only, no-peek, sealed-dir-verified):

- **SV1 (M3) — shrinkage backfire, closed-form.** Normal-normal conjugate: pool a new candidate (observed annualized Sharpe `y ≈ 1.5`, SE from `T`) with a hyperprior fit to the four cycles' leg Sharpes (mean ≈ −2.55). Compute the posterior mean; compare to a deploy threshold ∈ [1.0, 1.5]; sweep the hyperprior width. *Design-point preview (Codex):* posterior mean ≈ **−1.6 to −2.0** (most-generous variance −0.255) → below zero. SV1 formalizes + reports the width-sensitivity, and checks the advisor's sharpening (the only non-backfiring hyperprior collapses pooling into the per-candidate DSR-FWER already run = no-op).
- **SV2 (M2) — no free acceleration, tiny MC.** Does a pre-registered sequential posterior reach its deploy boundary for a *true* 1.5-Sharpe edge in ≤ the `T` the frequentist 80%-power MDE needs? Reuse `eval_power_mc.py` primitives; inject a known Sharpe; confirm the sequential rule does not beat the fixed-horizon power at matched `T` (and quantify any early-*kill* benefit, which is real).
- **SV3 (M1) — straddling-zero sizing ruin profile.** For the gate's measured ~20% detection, compute the fractional-Kelly the posterior implies and the sign-error / drawdown exposure. *Design-point preview (Codex):* `f* = 1.5/0.6 = 2.5×`, but `P(SR ≤ 0) ≈ 21%` at `se_ann ≈ 1.86` → fractional Kelly masks power as a sizing choice; under M3-pooling the implied Kelly can go negative (short the candidate).
- **SV4 (M4) — skeptical-prior direction.** Confirm a skeptical prior calibrated to the negatives can only *tighten* (never loosen) confirmation, and check whether it changes any SV1–SV3 conclusion (expected: no — it sharpens honesty, not power).
- **SV5 (M5) — e-value attainability.** What maximum e-value (or confidence-sequence half-width) does a true 1.5-Sharpe edge accrue over `forward_2026`'s `T`, and how much `T` to cross the α=0.05 threshold (`e ≥ 20`)? *Design-point preview (Codex):* expected log-LR ≈ **0.325 nats** vs the ≈ 3 nats needed → far short on this window → M5 is **only-via-time** on *power*, but its *decision-value* (optional-stopping-proof accrual) is assessed separately on Axis-2.
- **SV6 (M6) — change-the-estimand power comparison.** *The verdict-critical spot-check.* For a weak persistent directional edge consistent with a ~1–1.5 Sharpe, compute the power of a directional hit-rate / Brier-score test (binomial / proper-scoring) over the same `T` and compare to the Sharpe-DSR power on matched data. Does a different estimand yield materially higher detection without GoFP inflation? Closed-form binomial power + a tiny MC cross-check. This spot-check most directly drives BUILD vs DON'T-BUILD.
- **SV7 (M7) — winner's-curse EB, illustrative.** Conditional on a hypothetical future candidate batch, illustrate empirical-Bayes selection-bias correction; classify Axis-1/Axis-2 *conditional on a future batch being in scope* (flag the conditionality explicitly).

## 5. Pre-registered decision rule (set BEFORE the analysis — the anti-hindsight lock; **requires Charlie ratify**)

Each mechanism M1–M7 is classified on two axes; then a frozen verdict map applies.

- **Axis-1 — Power-legitimacy** (what happens to detection of a true ~1–1.5 edge; MECE on "detection at fixed `T`"):
  - `raises-power-legitimately` — increases detection on the **same non-holdout, fixed-`T`** data via a valid mechanism (e.g. a genuinely different estimand, valid pooling), **no** garden-of-forking-paths inflation.
  - `only-via-time` — increases detection **only** by consuming additional fresh OOS as `T` grows; **no** gain at fixed `T`.
  - `no-power-gain` — no detection improvement at fixed `T` and none beyond what raw waiting already gives for free.
  - `backfires` — **reduces** detection (e.g. shrinks toward a negative cohort).
- **Axis-2 — Decision-value** (independent of power: does it improve the *deploy decision* for a solo capital-protective researcher?): `high` / `modest` / `none` / `negative`. Examples of legitimate `high`: optional-stopping-proof honest accrual; reliable early-kill; correctly distinguishing under-power from no-edge in a way that changes a real action.

- **Verdict map (frozen pre-analysis; exhaustive + non-overlapping):**
  - **BUILD** ⟺ ∃ a mechanism classified `raises-power-legitimately`, **OR** ∃ a mechanism that is (`only-via-time` **AND** Axis-2 = `high`) — **and** that mechanism passes the anti-motivated-reasoning clause below.
  - **CONDITIONAL-BUILD ("go pending study X")** ⟺ a mechanism is *plausibly* `raises-power-legitimately` or (`only-via-time` AND `high`) on the reasoning, but the classification **genuinely hinges on a quantification exceeding the A⁺ inline budget** → name study X as the recommended next register.
  - **DON'T-BUILD** ⟺ every mechanism is {`no-power-gain` or `backfires`} on Axis-1 **AND** none reaches Axis-2 = `high`.
  - **Explicit residual-cell resolution (B2, Codex):** a mechanism that is (`only-via-time` AND Axis-2 = `modest`) contributes to **neither** BUILD nor CONDITIONAL — the power is free-from-waiting and the decision-value is sub-`high`, so it does **not** by itself justify a build. Absent any other rescuing mechanism, such a cell resolves to **DON'T-BUILD**.

- **Anti-motivated-reasoning clause (the hard pre-registration the freeze note demanded; two prongs):** a **BUILD** (or CONDITIONAL) recommendation must additionally pass BOTH:
  1. **Counterfactual-adoption prong (Codex).** The mechanism must have prior-art adoption as a principled improvement in a **neutral- or positive-cohort** setting (a citation / established practice), i.e. it would have been adopted **before** the four negatives were seen. A mechanism attractive *only because* it would rescue the dead cohort fails.
  2. **Parameterization-independence prong (advisor).** The mechanism's prior / decision-boundary **parameterization** must be pre-registered from a source **independent of the dead-cohort point estimates** — **except** where the parameterization can only make confirmation *stricter*. **Skeptical-prior directional exemption (adjudicated value-add):** a *skeptical* prior derived from the negatives is **permitted** (it self-penalizes — can only tighten confirmation); a *permissive* prior/boundary tuned to *rescue* candidates from the dead cohort is **blocked**.

- **Rationale (anti-hindsight).** The threshold for "modest deployable edge" is the ~1–1.5 annualized-Sharpe band (a good-but-realistic crypto-factor net Sharpe), consistent with the gate-power study. Pre-registering the axes + verdict map + clause *before* running the spot-checks guards against post-hoc verdict selection (choosing the rule to match the numbers after seeing them). The classifications and thresholds remain Charlie's to ratify or adjust.

## 7. Decision → next register mapping

- **DON'T-BUILD** (my held-loosely prior is this is *plausible* but not foregone): the deferred-open Bayesian-layer path is adjudicated closed *as a power lever*; the freeze's other deferred-open paths (fresh-OOS accrual, structurally-different frames) are untouched. A strategic confirmation that no evaluation re-framing rescues confirmation on the current frame.
- **CONDITIONAL-BUILD** (the live candidate — chiefly via M5/M2 decision-value or an M6 power surprise): name the next register — e.g. "pre-register a thin always-valid / e-value evidence-tracker for the accruing forward tape" (the *"build the honest ruler now, while there is no live candidate to game it"* argument the B2 elevated to first-class), or "a full estimand-power-comparison study (M6)."
- **BUILD** (requires an M6-style `raises-power-legitimately` that passes both clause prongs): a separate build register with its own pre-registration; still does not spend the holdout.

In all branches: scoping ≠ authorization. The build/study is a fresh Charlie register (anti-pre-emption).

Not a modification of `config/environments.yaml` or any sealed artifact. No peek at held-out performance. No spend of the 2024/2025 holdout. ## 9. Risks & assumptions (disclosed)

- **No-free-lunch is the load-bearing premise.** If the spot-checks somehow contradicted it (they should not — it is the gate-power study's measured result), the whole framing would need revisiting; SV2/SV5/SV6 are precisely the checks that could surface such a surprise.
- **M6 is the verdict-critical unknown.** A genuinely-higher-power estimand would flip toward BUILD/CONDITIONAL; SV6 must be run honestly (a different estimand can also be *lower* power or answer the wrong question). The held-loosely prior must not pre-empt SV6.
- **Anchoring risk (disclosed, both B2 legs + my own adjudication).** My composite prior leans DON'T-BUILD/CONDITIONAL — which conveniently agrees with the freeze it follows. The §5 lock + the requirement to *run* SV1–SV7 before classifying are the guard; the M3/M1 kills are hard (numerically grounded), the CONDITIONAL lean is soft (a judgment the reviewers could not ground in an artifact) and is weighted below the kills.
- **Garden-of-forking-paths at the meta-level.** Switching evaluators after four negatives is intrinsically suspect; the two-prong clause is the structural defense. If no mechanism clears both prongs, BUILD is unreachable by construction — which is the intended conservatism.
- **M7 conditionality.** Within-batch EB is only in scope if a future candidate batch is; otherwise it classifies as out-of-scope, not as a kill.
